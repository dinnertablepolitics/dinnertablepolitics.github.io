Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. In short: no cookies, no tracking unless you allow it, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Who runs this site

This website is a student project for the course Empirical Methods at the University of Zurich, run by Julian Hasler, Lilly Schiemann and Yuhan Ye under the team name Dinner Table Politics (dinnertablepolitics on GitHub). You can reach us through the issue tracker of our GitHub repository.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request to GitHub's servers. GitHub uses this to deliver the site and keep it secure, and may keep it in server logs. We do not have access to these logs. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. Fonts, pictures and scripts are served from this site itself, so no data goes to Google Fonts, social networks or advertising services.

Visitor counting is switched off. If we switch it on later, we will use GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data, and it will only run after you click “Allow counting” in a banner. Your choice is then saved in your browser's local storage so we do not ask again; you can change it with the “Analytics settings” link in the footer.

What you type on the page

The calculator and the quiz work entirely in your browser. Your slider positions and answers are not stored and not sent to anyone.

External links

Links to the paper, the data or GitHub take you to other sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may ask what personal data is processed about you and have it corrected or deleted. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, such requests about server logs need to go to GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
