What this site is

Dinner Table Politics explains and re-examines one published study, Cornelson (2026), “Politics at the Dinner Table: Thanksgiving and Social Influences on Political Polarisation”, The Economic Journal 136(675), 1125–1139. It was made by Julian Hasler, Lilly Schiemann and Yuhan Ye, students in the course Empirical Methods at the University of Zurich. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the author, the journal or the university.

No advice, no warranty

The content is for education only. We have checked our numbers carefully, but they come from our own replication and can contain mistakes. The calculator shows an average estimate for survey respondents, not a prediction about you or your family. Use the site at your own risk; we accept no liability for decisions based on it.
